---
title: "Persona-Aware Contextual Expressive TTS"
---
## ***Abstract***
Despite recent advances in expressive and conversational Text-to-Speech (TTS), existing approaches exhibit several limitations. Expressive TTS models typically rely on explicit style inputs or reference speech, while conversational TTS systems support limited interaction types and emotional expression. Moreover, neither paradigm accounts for speaker attributes that influence speech expression across contexts. To bridge the gap between expressive and conversational TTS, we introduce persona-aware contextual expressive TTS, a novel task that jointly incorporates speaker persona and dialog context for expressive speech synthesis. To support this task, we construct PACESpeech, a multi-speaker speech dataset annotated with persona attributes, comprising of 14,230 diverse utterances from 1,067 speakers and over 13 hours of speech. We propose PACE-TTS as a baseline model for this new task, consisting of an LLM-based speech style predictor and a TTS model pipeline for expressive speech generation. Through objective and subjective evaluations, we demonstrate the effectiveness of PACE-TTS over several baselines in generating natural and coherent expressive speech.
